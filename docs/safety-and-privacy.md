# Safety, privacy, and community rules

Users control cloud processing, telemetry, profile discovery, export, and deletion. Private conversations and memories are owner-scoped. Adult mode is disabled by default and can only be enabled after an age-gate confirms adulthood; it must remain isolated from public discovery and youth accounts. Provider adapters must apply moderation and policy checks before requests and responses.

## Community rules

Do not use EmberAI to harass people, exploit minors, impersonate without permission, distribute illegal content, or publish private information. Reports and blocks must be available before public/community features ship.

## Legal documents

Before launch, publish reviewed Terms of Service and a jurisdiction-appropriate Privacy Notice covering retention, processors, age assurance, deletion, and contact channels. This document is product guidance, not legal advice.
